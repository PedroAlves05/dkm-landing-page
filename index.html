Services

What we do

Rendering is where we started and what we're known for. Everything else is built around it, so the whole job can be finished by one team.

Rendering

Cement, acrylic and texture finishes on new builds, extensions and tired brickwork. Flat walls, consistent mix, sharp corners — no patchy sections and no hollow spots waiting to crack.

House painting

Usually run straight off the back of a render, so the whole house is finished by one crew without a hand-over gap between trades.

Venetian & polished plaster

Hand-burnished feature finishes with real depth in the surface. Slow, skilled work that can't be rushed and can't be faked with a roller.

Microcement

Seamless cement-based coating for floors and walls. No grout lines, no joins, no tile edges to catch dirt — one continuous surface, sealed and ready to live on.

Natural stone

Stone cladding laid for owners and architects who have already chosen their stone. You supply the material you fell in love with; we supply the labour, the adhesive, the grouting and the patience.

Full exterior renovation

Render, paint, stone and microcement packaged into a single run of work. One quote, one crew, one timeframe, and a house that looks like a different address at the end of it.

Why isn't DKM the cheapest quote?

Because the cheapest quote usually leaves something out — proper preparation, a decent product, or enough time to do it right. Those are the things that decide whether a render still looks good in a few years. We'd rather explain our price once than fix a wall twice.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. We come out, look at the job and give you a written scope with a finish date. No charge and no obligation.

How long will my job take?

It depends on the size and the finish, so we don't guess over the phone. You'll get a finish date in writing with your quote, before any work starts.

Do I need to do anything before you start?

Not much. We check access, water and power, then cover windows, floors and roof ourselves before any work begins.
